Broken street light? Tell us in under a minute.

Scan the QR code on the pole, pick the problem and send. We will notify you here as the repair moves along.

Report a street light Track a complaint

How it works

  1. Scan. Each pole carries a QR code with its ID and location.
  2. Report. Choose what is wrong and add a photo if you can.
  3. Get your ID. A complaint ID is created as soon as you submit.
  4. Follow updates. Notifications tell you when a technician is assigned and when it is fixed.

About this site

CityCare is the street lighting service of the fictional Greenfield Municipal Corporation. This is a demonstration website built for a computer science project. No real complaints are sent anywhere.
